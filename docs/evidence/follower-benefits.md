# Joined follower profession benefits

The existing follower policy reads current joined identities from `PartyFollowers` and profession facts from the one person catalog. Luck, resistance, experience learning, Merchant, Perception and Disarm Traps are derived at their actual combat, progression, service and interaction readers. Departure removes their reading immediately. No purchased skill/stat is changed, and the current save carries only canonical person identity/presence, rebuilding contributions from content.

Checked references: OpenEnroth `src/Engine/Objects/NPCEnums.h:27-90`, `Character.cpp:729-765` (Luck), `:1945-1993` (Enchanter resistance), `:625-641` (learning), `:2400-2547` (skill bonuses), and `src/Engine/PriceCalculator.cpp:134-158` (merchant quote). The operator's actual `npcprof.txt`, read through `NpcProfessionTable`, confirms Fool5, ChimneySweep20, PsychicLuck10/Perception5, Enchanter six protected kinds20, tutor percentages and Merchant/Disarm terms. The donor's Spirit damage uses Body resistance. Light/Dark/Physical/unresisted damage types get no Enchanter term.

This is the selected passive repertoire listed in the owning README. Distinct professions stack; duplicate people of a profession contribute once. These readings apply to actual joined hired/story presence in this product. Mastery remains purchased: a bonus does not grant a skill or a tier. Other original profession actions and bonuses are named uncompiled, including Scholar identification, Gypsy food/reputation and Burglar Stealing/reputation. No exhaustive profession fidelity is claimed, and existing fees and found-gold shares remain unchanged.
